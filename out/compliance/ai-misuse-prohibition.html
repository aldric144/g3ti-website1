ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY

AI MISUSE PROHIBITION

G3TI maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against the misuse of AI technology. This document outlines prohibited uses and our enforcement mechanisms.

"We will not develop, deploy, or enable AI systems that harm humanity."

Prohibited Uses

⚔️

Autonomous Weapons

AI systems designed to select and engage targets without meaningful human control

▼
👁️

Mass Surveillance

Indiscriminate monitoring of populations without legal basis or oversight

▼
🎭

Deceptive Manipulation

AI used to deceive, manipulate, or exploit human psychological vulnerabilities

▼
🚫

Discrimination

AI systems that discriminate based on protected characteristics

▼
🔓

Privacy Violations

Unauthorized collection, use, or disclosure of personal information

▼
💀

Harm to Individuals

AI applications that could cause physical, psychological, or financial harm

▼

Enforcement Process

1

Detection

Automated monitoring and human review identify potential misuse

2

Investigation

Security team conducts thorough investigation of the incident

3

Determination

Ethics committee reviews findings and determines violation severity

4

Action

Appropriate enforcement action taken based on violation type

5

Remediation

Steps taken to prevent recurrence and address any harm caused

Consequences

Minor Violations

  • • Written warning
  • • Mandatory retraining
  • • Enhanced monitoring
  • • Access restrictions

Serious Violations

  • • Service suspension
  • • Contract termination
  • • Financial penalties
  • • Public disclosure

Severe Violations

  • • Immediate termination
  • • Legal action
  • • Law enforcement referral
  • • Permanent ban
