OUR SACRED COMMITMENT

HUMAN PROTECTION PLEDGE

At G3TI, we believe that AI must serve humanity—not the other way around. This pledge represents our binding commitment to protect human dignity, safety, and rights in everything we build.

Our Pledge

1

We will never deploy AI systems that harm human life or dignity.

▼
2

We will maintain meaningful human oversight of all AI decisions.

▼
3

We will be transparent about AI capabilities and limitations.

▼
4

We will protect individual privacy and data rights.

▼
5

We will actively work to eliminate bias and discrimination.

▼
6

We will refuse to develop AI for autonomous weapons.

▼
7

We will support human workers affected by AI automation.

▼

Protection Areas

👶

Children

Special protections for minors in all AI interactions

  • Age verification
  • Content filtering
  • Parental controls
  • Data minimization
👴

Elderly

Safeguards against exploitation of vulnerable seniors

  • Scam detection
  • Simplified interfaces
  • Family alerts
  • Financial monitoring
🏥

Healthcare

Ethical AI in medical and health contexts

  • Clinical validation
  • Human oversight
  • Privacy protection
  • Bias testing
⚖️

Justice

Fair and unbiased AI in legal contexts

  • Algorithmic audits
  • Explainability
  • Appeal rights
  • Human review
💼

Employment

Protecting workers from AI-related harms

  • Bias-free hiring
  • Retraining support
  • Fair evaluation
  • Privacy rights
🏠

Housing

Preventing discrimination in housing decisions

  • Fair lending
  • Equal access
  • Bias monitoring
  • Transparency
