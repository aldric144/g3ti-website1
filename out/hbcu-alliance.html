Building Tomorrow's Intelligence Leaders

HBCU INTELLIGENCE ALLIANCE™

Building the Next Generation of AI, Intelligence, & Cyber Leadership.

HBCUs have always produced America's strongest thinkers, innovators, and protectors. From scientific pioneers to civil rights strategists, HBCUs have shaped the nation's moral and intellectual foundation.

Today, as AI-driven threats accelerate and the digital battlefield expands, a new generation of intelligence leaders is needed.

Global 3 Technology & Intelligence (G3TI) is honored to partner with Historically Black Colleges & Universities to build the future of protective intelligence, national security innovation, and AI research capacity.

Our Mission

To empower HBCU students, faculty, and research teams with the tools, training, and technology needed to lead America's next era of intelligence innovation.

We strengthen:

  • AI research capacity
  • Cybersecurity workforce development
  • National security innovation pathways
  • Intelligence & OSINT academic programs
  • Real-world protective intelligence skills

Why We Created This Alliance

AI-driven deception, digital exploitation, fraud networks, and emerging threat ecosystems disproportionately affect underserved communities.

G3TI believes:

"Intelligence must be created with the community, for the community, and led by the community."

By partnering with HBCUs, we honor the legacy of excellence and ensure African American students are not only protected by emerging technologies — but become the architects of those technologies.

Areas of Collaboration

  • AI & Intelligence Research Labs
  • Student Training & Certification Pathways
  • Internships & Workforce Pipelines
  • Real-World Intelligence Projects
  • Faculty Research Partnerships
