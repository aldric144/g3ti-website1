G3TI Autonomous Intelligence Engine - Overview

A multilayered, self-evolving intelligence system that processes millions of data points across deception, behavior, identity, and digital threat vectors.

1. Deception Intelligence Layer

The first line of defense. It detects:

  • AI-generated phishing
  • Deepfake impersonation
  • Synthetic communication anomalies
  • Fraud signatures
  • Coordinated manipulation signals

This layer identifies the intent behind the action - not just the action itself.

2. Behavioral Analysis Layer (Emotional-Forensic AI)

Modeled for:

  • Escalation prediction
  • Emotional volatility mapping
  • Crisis risk modeling
  • Insider threat profiling

This layer understands why a threat is emerging, not just how.

3. OSINT + Dark Data Fusion Layer

Aggregates:

  • Public data
  • Encrypted behavioral markers
  • Digital footprint patterns
  • Human trafficking signals
  • Domestic violence historical vectors

Fuses all sources into a single threat graph.

4. Predictive Risk Pipeline

Forecasts:

  • Escalation probability
  • Likely target
  • Likely timeline
  • Likely operational method

Outputs early-stage warnings for government, law enforcement, and enterprise administrators.

5. Autonomous Response Intelligence

Automates:

  • Notifications
  • Reports
  • Threat triage
  • Risk rankings
  • Correlation summaries

Each sector receives its own tailored intelligence output.
